about this site

the history of the world is a small, simple website that tries to tell the story of everything, from the start of the universe to today, in plain words that anyone can follow.

what is this?

this site is a starting point, not an encyclopedia. the full history of the world could fill millions of books, so every part here is a short summary of the biggest events, people, and ideas. if something catches your interest, go and read more about it.

why no dates?

this site tells history as a story, in order, without dates. the parts go from first to last, and within each page, things mostly go from earlier to later. when different places are covered on the same page, they happened around the same time.

how it is made

  • plain html, written by hand.
  • one small stylesheet.
  • no javascript, no cookies, no tracking, no ads.
  • it should load fast on any computer and any connection.

a note on fairness

history is huge, messy, and argued about. this site tries to cover every part of the world, not just one region, and to be honest about the bad parts as well as the good. some things are left out simply to keep it short. historians also still disagree about many details, and new discoveries change what we know all the time.
