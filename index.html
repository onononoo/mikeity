welcome to the history of the world

this site tells the whole story of the world, from the very first moment of the universe all the way to the world we live in now. it is split into eleven parts. each part is one big chapter of the story, and each one leads into the next.

you do not need to know anything to start. just click on part 1 and keep going. if you only care about one period, jump straight to it using the table below or the menu on the left.

the eleven parts

#partwhat happens
1the beginningthe universe appears, stars and planets form, the earth cools, and life begins in the oceans.
2the first humansearly human ancestors walk upright, learn to use fire and tools, and spread out across the planet.
3the first farmerspeople settle down, plant crops, tame animals, and build the first villages.
4the first civilizationscities, kings, writing, and laws appear along great rivers in mesopotamia, egypt, india, and china.
5the classical worldpersia, greece, rome, india, and china build huge empires and big ideas about how to live.
6the middle agesrome falls in the west, islam rises, the mongols conquer, and great kingdoms grow in africa and the americas.
7the early modern worldthe printing press, ocean voyages, the reformation, and the first truly global connections.
8the age of revolutionsnew ideas about freedom lead to revolutions in america, france, haiti, and latin america.
9industry and empiremachines and factories change everything, and european empires take over much of the world.
10the world warstwo enormous wars, a great depression, and the holocaust.
11the modern worldthe cold war, the end of empires, space travel, computers, the internet, and the world today.

how to read this site

  • each part starts with a short yellow box that sums it up.
  • each part has a quick facts table at the bottom.
  • use the links at the bottom of each page to go to the previous or next part.
  • the whole site is in plain words. no big textbook language.

a note on time

this site does not use dates. instead, the story is told in order, from first to last. things near the top of a page happened before things near the bottom, and each part comes after the one before it. when things happened at the same time in different places, the page says so.
