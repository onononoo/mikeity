# religion: islam. muslims add a blessing after the names of the prophets; the site writes names plainly.

== creation {#creation}

the [[quran]] teaches that [[allah]], the one god, created the heavens and the earth in six days. god has only to say "be", and it is. he made the angels from light and the [[jinn]] from smokeless fire, and then he made humankind.

god shaped [[@adam]] from clay, breathed his spirit into him, and taught him the names of all things. he told the angels to bow to adam. all did except iblis, who refused out of pride and became satan, the tempter. adam and his wife, hawwa, ate from the one tree god had forbidden. they repented, and god forgave them, then sent them down to earth. in islam there is no inherited sin: every person is born pure and is responsible for their own deeds.

the quran gives no date for creation. it says that a day with god can be like a thousand years, or fifty thousand, and many muslims see no conflict with the scientific age of the universe.

== the prophets {#prophets}

muslims believe god sent a [[prophet]] to every people, each with the same message: worship the one god and do good. the quran names 25 of them, many shared with the jewish and christian bibles. [[@nuh]] warned his people before the flood. [[@ibrahim]] turned from idols and, with his son [[@ismail]], built the [[kaaba]] in mecca. [[@musa]] freed the children of israel from pharaoh and received the tawrat. dawud received the psalms. [[@isa]], born by a miracle to [[@maryam]], received the gospel. but each message, muslims believe, was later forgotten or changed, until god sent a final prophet.

== the life of the prophet {#muhammad}

[[@muhammad]] was born in mecca around 570, orphaned young, and grew up to be a trusted merchant who married [[@khadija]]. in 610, while praying in a cave on mount hira, he was visited by the angel jibril, who brought the first words of the quran: "read, in the name of your lord who created." for 23 years the revelations continued.

his message, that there is one god and that the rich must care for the poor, angered the leaders of mecca, and his followers were persecuted. in 622 they moved to medina, the [[hijra]], which begins the islamic calendar. there muhammad led a new community, the [[ummah]]. after years of war, mecca surrendered in 630 and the idols were cleared from the kaaba. muhammad died in 632, having united most of arabia.

== what historians can show {#history}

historians know more about the life of muhammad than about the founders of most religions, though the fullest biographies were written down more than a century after his death. many scholars accept that the text of the quran was fixed very early, under the caliph uthman around 650, and inscriptions and early manuscripts support this.

== the caliphs and the great split {#split}

after muhammad, the community was led by [[caliph|caliphs]]: [[@abu bakr]], [[@umar]], uthman, and [[@ali ibn abi talib]]. within a century arab armies had conquered an empire from spain to central asia. a dispute over who should lead became the split between [[sunni]] and [[shia]] muslims, sealed by the killing of the prophet's grandson [[@husayn ibn ali]] at karbala in 680. shia muslims follow a line of [[imam|imams]] descended from ali and [[@fatima]].

== a civilization {#civilization}

under the abbasids, baghdad became one of the great centers of learning in the world. scholars such as [[@al-bukhari]] gathered the [[hadith]], and jurists built up [[sharia]], the islamic way of life and law. mystics like [[@rabia of basra]] and later [[@rumi]] followed the path of [[sufism]], and [[@al-ghazali]] brought law and mysticism together. islam spread less by conquest than by trade: merchants, scholars, and sufis carried it across the sahara to rulers like [[@mansa musa]], along the swahili coast, and across the indian ocean to indonesia. [[@ibn battuta]] could travel from morocco to china and find muslim communities almost all the way.

== islam today {#today}

today about two billion people are muslim, about one person in four on earth. most live in asia and africa, and only about one in five is arab. indonesia, pakistan, india, and bangladesh have the most muslims. like other faiths, islam includes a great range of practice, from strict to liberal, and of cultures, from senegal to china.

== belief and practice {#practice}

- one god, the angels, the holy books, the prophets, the last day, and god's decree.
- the [[five pillars]]: the [[shahada]], [[salah]] five times a day, [[zakat]], fasting in [[ramadan]], and the [[hajj]].
- prayer in the [[mosque]], especially at noon on friday.
- the festivals of [[eid]], and the example of the prophet, the [[sunnah]].
