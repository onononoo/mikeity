people of islam

21 people, in the order they appear in the story. figures marked tradition are known from scripture and teaching; those marked history are known from historical records as well. click a column heading to sort.

1adamthe first humantraditionthe first prophet. made by god from clay and taught the names of all things; he repented and was forgiven
2nuhbefore the floodtraditionprophet (noah). called his people to god for centuries and was saved from the flood in the ark
3ibrahimlong before musatraditionprophet (abraham), "the friend of god". turned from idols to the one god and built the kaaba with his son ismail; the hajj retraces his family's story
4ismailson of ibrahimtraditionprophet (ishmael). the son whom ibrahim was ready to sacrifice, remembered at eid al-adha; ancestor of the arabs of mecca
5musathe time of pharaohtraditionprophet (moses). the prophet named most often in the quran; he spoke with god and received the tawrat
6maryammother of isatraditionthe mother of jesus. the only woman named in the quran, which has a chapter called by her name
7isabefore muhammadtraditionprophet and messiah (jesus). born of a virgin and given the injil; muslims believe he will return before the last day
8khadijac. 555-619historywife of muhammad. a successful merchant and the first person to accept islam
9muhammadc. 570-632historythe prophet of islam. received the quran over 23 years and united arabia under islam; muslims call him the seal of the prophets (also on the main people page.)
10abu bakrc. 573-634historythe first caliph. the prophet's closest friend, who led the community after his death
11umarc. 584-644historythe second caliph. led the conquests of syria, egypt, and persia and set up the islamic calendar
12ali ibn abi talibc. 600-661historycousin and son-in-law of muhammad, the fourth caliph. shia muslims regard him as the first imam and the prophet's rightful successor
13fatimac. 605-632historydaughter of muhammad. wife of ali and mother of hasan and husayn, deeply honored by all muslims
14aishac. 613-678historywife of muhammad. one of the most important sources of hadith and a teacher of the early community
15husayn ibn ali626-680historygrandson of muhammad. killed at karbala; his death is mourned every year by shia muslims on ashura
16rabia of basrac. 714-801historysufi saint. taught that god should be loved for himself alone, not from fear of hell or hope of paradise
17al-bukhari810-870historyscholar of hadith. is said to have checked some 600,000 reports to choose about 7,000 for his collection
18al-ghazali1058-1111historytheologian and mystic. left a famous teaching post in baghdad in a spiritual crisis and later wrote the revival of the religious sciences
19rumi1207-1273historypersian poet and sufi. his poems of love for god are read around the world; his followers became the whirling dervishes (also on the main people page.)
20mansa musac. 1280-1337historyemperor of mali. built mosques and schools in timbuktu after his famous pilgrimage (also on the main people page.)
21ibn battuta1304-1369historytraveler and judge. journeyed across the islamic world from morocco to china and wrote about it (also on the main people page.)
