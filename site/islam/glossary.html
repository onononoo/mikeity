islam: glossary

words used in islam (23 terms). words linked in the story point here. the main glossary has the rest.

a · c · e · f · h · i · j · k · m · p · q · r · s · u · z

a

allah
the arabic word for god, used by arab christians and jews as well as muslims.

c

caliph
"successor": the leader of the muslim community after muhammad.

e

eid
a festival: eid al-fitr at the end of ramadan, and eid al-adha at the time of the hajj.

f

five pillars
the five duties of every muslim: the shahada, prayer (salah), charity (zakat), fasting in ramadan, and the hajj. see also: shahada, salah, zakat, ramadan, hajj.

h

hadith
a report of something the prophet said or did, passed down through a chain of named people. see also: sunnah.
hajj
the pilgrimage to mecca, which every muslim who is able should make once in their life. see also: kaaba.
hijra
the move of muhammad and his followers from mecca to medina in 622, year 1 of the islamic calendar.

i

imam
one who leads prayer; for shia muslims, the divinely guided leaders descended from ali. see also: shia.

j

jinn
beings made of smokeless fire, with free will like humans, mostly unseen.

k

kaaba
the cube-shaped shrine at the center of the great mosque of mecca, which muslims face when they pray. see also: hajj.

m

mosque
a muslim place of prayer, in arabic masjid, "place of bowing down".

p

prophet
a messenger of god. the quran names 25, from adam to muhammad, the last.

q

quran
"the recitation": the holy book of islam, which muslims believe is the word of god revealed to muhammad in arabic.

r

ramadan
the ninth month of the islamic year, when muslims fast from dawn to sunset. see also: eid.

s

salah
the ritual prayer, said five times a day facing mecca. see also: five pillars.
shahada
the declaration of faith: "there is no god but god, and muhammad is the messenger of god." see also: five pillars.
sharia
"the way": islamic law and ethics, worked out by scholars from the quran and sunnah.
shia
the second largest branch of islam, about a tenth to an eighth of muslims, who follow the line of ali and the imams. see also: sunni, imam.
sufism
the mystical path in islam, seeking closeness to god through prayer, remembrance, music, and poetry.
sunnah
the example of the prophet's words and life, known from the hadith. see also: hadith.
sunni
the largest branch of islam, followed by most muslims, who accept the first four caliphs in order. see also: shia.

u

ummah
the worldwide community of all muslims.

z

zakat
the yearly giving of a share of one's wealth to the poor and needy. see also: five pillars.
