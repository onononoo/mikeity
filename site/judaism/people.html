people of judaism

22 people, in the order they appear in the story. figures marked tradition are known from scripture and teaching; those marked history are known from historical records as well. click a column heading to sort.

1adamthe first mantraditionthe first human. formed by god from the dust of the earth and placed in the garden of eden
2evethe first womantraditionthe first woman. "the mother of all living", who ate the forbidden fruit with adam
3noahbefore and after the floodtraditionbuilder of the ark. a righteous man saved with his family from the flood; god made a covenant with him and all living things
4abraham1813-1638 bce, by traditiontraditionthe first patriarch. left his homeland at god's call; the covenant with him is the beginning of the jewish people
5sarahwife of abrahamtraditionthe first matriarch. gave birth to isaac in old age, as god had promised
6jacobgrandson of abrahamtraditionthe third patriarch, also called israel. wrestled with an angel and was renamed israel; his twelve sons became the twelve tribes
7josephson of jacobtraditiondreamer and minister of egypt. sold into slavery by his brothers, he rose to rule egypt under pharaoh and saved his family from famine
8moses1393-1273 bce, by traditiontraditionprophet, lawgiver, and leader of the exodus. led the israelites out of egypt and received the torah at sinai; the greatest of the prophets
9miriamsister of mosestraditionprophet. led the women in song after the crossing of the sea
10davidc. 1040-970 bce, by the biblical accounttraditionking of israel. shepherd, poet of the psalms, and king who made jerusalem his capital; the messiah is to be his descendant
11solomonreigned c. 970-931 bce, by the biblical accounttraditionking of israel. famous for his wisdom; built the first temple in jerusalem
12elijah9th century bce, by the biblical accounttraditionprophet. challenged the worship of baal and was carried to heaven in a chariot of fire; a cup is poured for him at passover
13esther5th century bce, by the biblical accounttraditionqueen of persia. saved the jews of persia from destruction, remembered at the festival of purim
14hillelc. 110 bce-10 cehistorysage in jerusalem. summed up the torah as "what is hateful to you, do not do to your neighbor; the rest is commentary"
15rabbi akivac. 50-135 cehistorysage and teacher. one of the great founders of rabbinic judaism, put to death by the romans after the bar kokhba revolt
16judah ha-nasic. 135-217 cehistorypatriarch of the jews in roman palestine. compiled the mishnah, the first written collection of the oral law
17rashi1040-1105historyrabbi in troyes, france. wrote the most widely read commentaries on the bible and talmud
18maimonidesc. 1138-1204historyphilosopher, legal scholar, and doctor. wrote the mishneh torah and the guide for the perplexed; "from moses to moses there was none like moses" (also on the main people page.)
19baal shem tovc. 1698-1760historyfounder of hasidism. taught that god can be served with joy in every part of life, not only through study
20moses mendelssohn1729-1786historygerman jewish philosopher. led the haskalah, the jewish enlightenment, and argued for religious freedom
21theodor herzl1860-1904historyjournalist and founder of political zionism. wrote the jewish state and called the first zionist congress
22anne frank1929-1945historydiarist. hid from the nazis in amsterdam and died in bergen-belsen; her diary is read around the world (also on the main people page.)
