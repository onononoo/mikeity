the timeline of hinduism

25 entries, in order. rows marked tradition tell the story as hinduism itself tells it, with traditional dates where there are any; rows marked history are what historians can show. compare the main timeline.

1before creationtradition"then there was neither being nor non-being." the creation hymn of the rigveda asks how it all began, and ends: perhaps even the one who watches from the highest heaven does not know
2the dawn of a kalpatraditionvishnu sleeps on the great serpent shesha, floating on the cosmic ocean. a lotus grows from his navel, and on it brahma is born and creates the worlds
3every day of brahmatraditiona day of brahma, a kalpa, lasts 4.32 billion years, and his night as long. at night the worlds dissolve; at dawn they are made again. after a hundred years of such days, all dissolves and a new brahma is born
4the first floodtraditionvishnu, as the fish matsya, warns manu of a great flood and tows his boat to safety with the seeds of all life and the seven sages
5satya yuga: 1,728,000 yearstraditionthe age of truth. dharma stands firm on four legs; people are good, wise, and live very long lives
6treta yuga: 1,296,000 yearstraditiondharma stands on three legs. rama, the seventh avatar of vishnu, rescues his wife sita from the demon king ravana: the story of the ramayana
7dvapara yuga: 864,000 yearstraditiondharma stands on two legs. krishna, the eighth avatar, is born; he guides arjuna in the great war at kurukshetra, and his teaching becomes the bhagavad gita
83102 bce, by traditiontraditionkrishna leaves the world and the kali yuga begins: the age of strife, 432,000 years long, in which we live now
9c. 2600–1900 bcehistorythe indus cities flourish; some of their seals show figures in yoga-like poses, but how they relate to later hinduism is debated
10c. 1500–1200 bcehistorythe hymns of the rigveda are composed in early sanskrit and passed down by memory, word for word, for many centuries
11c. 800–200 bcehistorythe upanishads explore the self (atman), ultimate reality (brahman), karma, and rebirth
12c. 400 bce–400 cehistorythe great epics, the mahabharata and the ramayana, take their final form; the bhagavad gita becomes part of the mahabharata
13c. 320–550 cehistoryin the gupta age, the puranas are written, and the first stone temples to vishnu, shiva, and the goddess are built
14c. 600–900 cehistorytamil poet-saints, the alvars and nayanars, sing of loving devotion (bhakti) to vishnu and shiva
15c. 800 cehistoryadi shankara teaches that the self and brahman are one (advaita), and founds monasteries at the four corners of india
16c. 1100historyramanuja teaches that loving devotion to vishnu leads to liberation
17c. 1113–1150historythe khmer king suryavarman ii builds angkor wat in cambodia, a vast temple to vishnu
18c. 1510historychaitanya leads ecstatic singing of krishna's names in bengal; in rajasthan, mirabai writes songs of love for krishna
191574historytulsidas begins the ramcharitmanas, a retelling of the ramayana in hindi, still recited across north india
201828historyram mohan roy founds the brahmo samaj, calling for reform and the worship of one god
211893historyswami vivekananda speaks at the parliament of the world's religions in chicago, bringing hindu teaching to the west
221947historyindia becomes independent as a secular republic; partition divides hindus and muslims with great bloodshed
231966historya. c. bhaktivedanta swami prabhupada founds the hare krishna movement in new york
24todayhistoryabout 1.2 billion hindus live mainly in india and nepal; the kumbh mela, held every twelve years at prayagraj, is often called the largest gathering of people on earth
25the end of the kali yugatraditionkalki, the tenth avatar of vishnu, will come on a white horse to end the age of darkness, and a new satya yuga will begin
