7krishnadied 3102 bce, by traditiontraditionavatar of vishnu. a playful cowherd, a hero, and the teacher of the bhagavad gita; for many hindus the supreme god himself

10ramanujac. 1017-1137, by traditionhistoryphilosopher and teacher. taught that the soul is real and distinct, and is saved by loving devotion to vishnu
11chaitanya1486-1534historysaint of bengal. spread devotion to krishna through chanting and dancing in the streets
12mirabaic. 1498-1547historyprincess and poet-saint. defied her royal family to devote her life to krishna; her songs are still sung
13tulsidasc. 1532-1623historypoet. wrote the ramcharitmanas, the best-loved book in north india
14ram mohan roy1772-1833historyreformer. founded the brahmo samaj and campaigned against the burning of widows (also on the main people page.)
15ramakrishna1836-1886historymystic of kolkata. taught that all religions are different paths to the same god
16swami vivekananda1863-1902historymonk and teacher. a follower of ramakrishna who brought vedanta and yoga to the west
17mahatma gandhi1869-1948historyleader of india's independence movement. drew on the bhagavad gita for his teaching of non-violence and truth (also on the main people page.)
18a. c. bhaktivedanta swami prabhupada1896-1977historymonk and teacher. went to new york at 69 and founded the international society for krishna consciousness
