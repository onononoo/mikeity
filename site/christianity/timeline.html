14004 bce, by ussher's reckoningtraditiongod creates the world in six days and makes humans in his image, as told in genesis. the date is archbishop ussher's calculation of 1650, not a teaching of the church
2the falltraditionadam and eve disobey god in the garden of eden; sin and death enter the world, and god promises that evil will one day be defeated
3the old testamenttraditionthe story of israel, from abraham and moses to david and the prophets, which christians read as preparing the way for the messiah
4c. 4 bcetraditionthe angel gabriel tells mary she will bear the son of god. jesus is born in bethlehem; angels sing to shepherds and wise men follow a star
5c. 4 bcehistoryjesus of nazareth is born, probably shortly before the death of king herod in 4 bce; the monk who later fixed the year 1 was a few years out
6c. 28 cetraditionjohn the baptist baptizes jesus in the jordan. jesus begins teaching, healing the sick, and calls twelve disciples
7c. 30 cehistoryjesus is crucified in jerusalem under the roman governor pontius pilate, an event also recorded by the roman historian tacitus
8the third daytraditionjesus rises from the dead, appears to mary magdalene and the disciples, and forty days later ascends into heaven
9pentecosttraditionthe holy spirit comes down on the apostles in jerusalem, and they begin preaching; christians see this as the birth of the church
10c. 35 cehistorypaul, who had persecuted the first christians, becomes one himself after a vision on the road to damascus
11c. 50 cehistoryat a meeting in jerusalem, the apostles agree that gentiles who become christians need not follow the whole jewish law
12c. 50–60 cehistorypaul writes letters to the churches he founded, the oldest christian writings to survive
1364 cehistorythe emperor nero blames christians for the great fire of rome; by tradition peter and paul are put to death there
14c. 65–100 cehistorythe four gospels, of mark, matthew, luke, and john, are written
15303–311 cehistorythe great persecution under diocletian, the last and worst attempt by rome to wipe out christianity
16313 cehistoryconstantine and licinius issue the edict of milan, making christianity legal
17325 cehistorythe council of nicaea agrees the nicene creed: the son is "of one being with the father"
18380 cehistorychristianity becomes the official religion of the roman empire
19c. 405 cehistoryjerome finishes translating the bible into latin, the vulgate, used in the western church for a thousand years
20426 cehistoryaugustine of hippo finishes the city of god, written after the sack of rome
21c. 529 cehistorybenedict founds the monastery of monte cassino; his rule shapes western monks and nuns
221054historythe great schism divides the catholic west from the orthodox east
231095historypope urban ii calls the first crusade
241209historyfrancis of assisi gathers his first followers, who live in poverty and preach
251517historymartin luther's 95 theses begin the protestant reformation
261536historyjohn calvin publishes the institutes of the christian religion in geneva
271540historythe pope approves the jesuits, founded by ignatius of loyola; they soon run schools and missions worldwide
281545–1563historythe council of trent leads the catholic counter-reformation
291611historythe king james bible is published in english
301738historyjohn wesley's conversion begins the methodist revival in britain and america
311962–1965historythe second vatican council modernizes the catholic church; mass is now said in local languages
32todayhistorymost christians now live in africa, latin america, and asia, not in europe and north america
33the end of timetraditionchrist will come again in glory; the dead will rise, the world will be judged, and god will make a new heaven and a new earth
