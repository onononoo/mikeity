people of christianity

1mary1st century bce-1st century cetraditionthe mother of jesus. honored by most christians as the mother of god and by catholics and orthodox as queen of heaven
2john the baptistdied c. 30 cehistoryprophet and preacher. baptized jesus in the jordan; beheaded by herod antipas, as the historian josephus also records
3jesus of nazarethc. 4 bce-30 cehistoryteacher from galilee, whom christians worship as the son of god. preached the coming of the kingdom of god, was crucified in jerusalem, and, christians believe, rose from the dead (also on the main people page.)
4mary magdalene1st century cetraditionfollower of jesus. the gospels say she was the first to see the risen jesus
5peterdied c. 64 cehistoryleader of the twelve apostles. a fisherman from galilee; catholics see him as the first pope, and by tradition he was crucified in rome
6paul of tarsusc. 5-64 cehistoryapostle to the gentiles. spread christianity across the eastern mediterranean; his letters make up much of the new testament (also on the main people page.)
7constantinec. 272-337historyroman emperor. the first christian emperor; he called the council of nicaea (also on the main people page.)
8jeromec. 342-420historyscholar and translator. translated the bible into latin, the vulgate
9augustine of hippo354-430historybishop in north africa. his confessions and city of god shaped western christian thought more than any writer after paul
10benedict of nursiac. 480-547historymonk. wrote the rule of saint benedict, the guide for most western monasteries
11francis of assisic. 1181-1226historyfriar. gave up his wealth to live in poverty and serve the poor; loved all creatures as his brothers and sisters
12thomas aquinas1225-1274historydominican friar and theologian. joined christian teaching with the philosophy of aristotle in his summa theologiae (also on the main people page.)
13martin luther1483-1546historygerman monk and reformer. taught that people are saved by faith alone and translated the bible into german (also on the main people page.)
14ignatius of loyola1491-1556historyspanish priest. founded the jesuits and wrote the spiritual exercises
15john calvin1509-1564historyfrench reformer in geneva. his teaching shaped the reformed and presbyterian churches
16john wesley1703-1791historyenglish preacher. rode some 400,000 kilometers preaching in the open air; founder of methodism
17mother teresa1910-1997historycatholic nun. cared for the dying poor of kolkata and founded the missionaries of charity
18martin luther king jr.1929-1968historybaptist minister and civil rights leader. drew on the bible and the example of jesus to lead non-violent protest against segregation (also on the main people page.)
19desmond tutu1931-2021historyanglican archbishop of cape town. fought apartheid without violence and chaired south africa's truth and reconciliation commission
