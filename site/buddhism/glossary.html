buddhism: glossary

words used in buddhism (19 terms). words linked in the story point here. the main glossary has the rest.

a · b · d · e · f · k · m · n · s · t · v · z

a

anatta
"not-self": the teaching that there is no fixed, unchanging self inside a person. see also: dukkha.

b

bodhisattva
a being on the way to buddhahood; in mahayana, one who puts off final nirvana to help all beings. see also: mahayana.
buddha
"awakened one": someone who has fully understood the truth. the buddha of our age is siddhartha gautama.

d

dalai lama
the leading teacher of the gelug school of tibetan buddhism, believed to be reborn in each generation. see also: vajrayana.
dharma
in buddhism, the teaching of the buddha and the truth it points to. see also: sangha.
dukkha
suffering, or the unsatisfactory nature of life, the first of the four noble truths. see also: four noble truths.

e

eightfold path
the way to end suffering: right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, and concentration. see also: four noble truths.

f

four noble truths
life involves suffering; suffering comes from craving; it can end; and the eightfold path is the way. see also: eightfold path, dukkha.

k

kalpa
an eon, a span of time so long it can only be described by comparisons.
karma
intentional action, which shapes a person's future in this life and in rebirth.

m

mahayana
"the great vehicle": the branch of buddhism of china, korea, japan, and vietnam, centered on the bodhisattva. see also: theravada, vajrayana.
meditation
training the mind in calm and insight, at the heart of buddhist practice.

n

nirvana
"blowing out": the end of craving and suffering, and freedom from rebirth.

s

sangha
the community of monks and nuns, and in a wider sense all buddhists. see also: dharma.
stupa
a domed monument holding relics of the buddha or a saint.
sutra
a discourse of the buddha, or a text believed to record his teaching.

t

theravada
"the way of the elders": the branch of buddhism of sri lanka, myanmar, thailand, laos, and cambodia. see also: mahayana.

v

vajrayana
"the diamond vehicle": the buddhism of tibet, mongolia, and bhutan, with special rituals and teachers. see also: mahayana.

z

zen
a school of mahayana buddhism, chan in chinese, that stresses meditation and sudden insight.
