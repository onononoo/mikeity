1beginningless timetraditionthe buddha teaches that no first beginning of the round of rebirth can be found. world systems without number arise, last, decline, and pass away
2every great kalpatraditioneach world passes through four long stages: forming, lasting, falling apart, and emptiness, and then forms again
3countless eons agotraditionthe ascetic sumedha meets the buddha dipankara and vows to become a buddha himself. over countless lives he perfects generosity, patience, and wisdom, as told in the jataka tales
4in the tushita heaventraditionthe future buddha waits in heaven for the right time, then chooses to be born as prince siddhartha
5the birthtraditionqueen maya gives birth to siddhartha in the lumbini garden. the baby takes seven steps, and a sage foretells he will be either a great king or a buddha
6c. 480 bcehistorymost historians place the buddha's birth around 480 bce at lumbini, in modern nepal; the theravada tradition dates it to 623 bce
7at the age of 29traditionoutside the palace, siddhartha sees an old man, a sick man, a corpse, and a wandering holy man. he leaves his family and his wealth to seek the end of suffering
8at the age of 35traditionsitting under the bodhi tree at bodh gaya, he overcomes the tempter mara and wakes up to the truth: he is now the buddha
9the first sermontraditionin the deer park at sarnath, the buddha teaches the four noble truths to five companions, who become the first monks
10at the age of 80traditionafter 45 years of teaching, the buddha passes into final nirvana at kushinagar
11c. 400 bcehistoryaccording to tradition, 500 monks meet to recite the teachings together; they are passed down by memory for centuries
12c. 260 bcehistorythe emperor ashoka turns to buddhism after the slaughter at kalinga and sends teachers across and beyond india, including his son mahinda to sri lanka
13c. 29 bcehistorymonks in sri lanka write down the teachings in the pali language, the pali canon of theravada buddhism
14c. 65 cehistorybuddhism reaches china along the silk roads; by tradition the white horse temple is founded at luoyang
15c. 100 cehistorymahayana sutras, such as the lotus sutra and the perfection of wisdom, appear, teaching the path of the bodhisattva
16c. 150–250 cehistorynagarjuna teaches that all things are empty of fixed, separate existence
17372 cehistorybuddhism reaches korea
185th centuryhistorythe monastery university of nalanda is founded in india; it will draw students from all over asia
19538 cehistorya korean king sends an image of the buddha and sutras to japan
20629–645 cehistorythe chinese monk xuanzang walks to india to collect scriptures and brings back hundreds of texts
21c. 779 cehistorysamye, the first buddhist monastery in tibet, is completed
22c. 800 cehistorythe great stupa of borobudur is built on java
23868 cehistorythe diamond sutra is printed in china, the oldest dated printed book
24c. 1193historyinvaders destroy nalanda; buddhism fades away in most of india
251227historydogen returns from china and brings soto zen to japan
261642historythe fifth dalai lama becomes the ruler of tibet
271956historyb. r. ambedkar and about half a million dalits become buddhists at nagpur, rejecting caste
281959historythe fourteenth dalai lama flees chinese rule in tibet and settles in india
29todayhistoryabout 500 million buddhists live mostly in east and southeast asia; buddhist meditation has spread worldwide
30in the distant futuretraditionwhen the teaching has been forgotten, maitreya, the next buddha, will be born and teach the path again
