7c. 1500-1521traditionguru nanak makes long journeys with the musician mardana, across india and, by tradition, to tibet, sri lanka, mecca, and baghdad
8c. 1521historyguru nanak settles at kartarpur, where his followers farm, pray, sing, and eat together
91539historyguru nanak dies, naming his disciple angad as the second guru; guru angad spreads the gurmukhi script
101552–1574historyguru amar das insists that everyone, rich or poor, eat together in the langar before meeting him
111577historyguru ram das founds the city of amritsar around a sacred pool
121604historyguru arjan compiles the adi granth, the sikh scripture, and installs it in the harmandir sahib at amritsar
131606historyguru arjan is tortured and put to death on the orders of the mughal emperor jahangir, the first sikh martyr
141606historyguru hargobind wears two swords, for spiritual and worldly power, and builds the akal takht
151675historyguru tegh bahadur is beheaded in delhi on aurangzeb's orders after defending the right of hindus to keep their faith
161699historyat the vaisakhi festival, guru gobind singh founds the khalsa, the order of initiated sikhs, with the five beloved ones
171708historybefore he dies, guru gobind singh declares that the scripture, the guru granth sahib, will be the guru forever
181716historybanda singh bahadur, who led a sikh uprising against mughal rule, is captured and executed in delhi
191799historyranjit singh takes lahore and goes on to build the sikh empire
20c. 1830historyunder ranjit singh the upper floors of the harmandir sahib are covered in gold, and it becomes known as the golden temple
211849historyafter two wars, the british conquer the punjab
221919historybritish troops fire on a crowd gathered in the jallianwala bagh at amritsar on vaisakhi day, killing hundreds
231947historypartition splits the punjab between india and pakistan; millions of sikhs leave their homes in the west
241984historythe indian army storms the golden temple; after indira gandhi is killed by her sikh bodyguards, thousands of sikhs are murdered in riots
25todayhistoryabout 25 to 30 million sikhs live mostly in the punjab, with large communities in britain, canada, and the united states
