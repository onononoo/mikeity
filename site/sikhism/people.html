people of sikhism

15 people, in the order they appear in the story. figures marked tradition are known from scripture and teaching; those marked history are known from historical records as well. click a column heading to sort.

#namelivedaccountwho they were
1guru nanak1469-1539historythe first guru and founder of sikhism. taught devotion to the one god, honest work, sharing, and the equality of all people (also on the main people page.)
2bhai mardanac. 1459-1534historymusician. a muslim who played the rebab and traveled with guru nanak for decades, setting his hymns to music
3guru angad1504-1552historythe second guru. spread the gurmukhi script and wrestling and exercise for young sikhs
4mata khivic. 1506-1582historywife of guru angad. ran the langar, the free kitchen, and is the only woman named in the guru granth sahib
5guru amar das1479-1574historythe third guru. became guru at 73; spoke against caste, the veiling of women, and the burning of widows
6guru ram das1534-1581historythe fourth guru. founded amritsar and wrote the hymn sung at sikh weddings
7guru arjan1563-1606historythe fifth guru. compiled the adi granth, built the harmandir sahib with doors on all four sides, and was martyred
8guru hargobind1595-1644historythe sixth guru. taught that sikhs must be both saints and soldiers
9guru har rai1630-1661historythe seventh guru. known for his gentleness and for keeping herbal medicine gardens
10guru har krishan1656-1664historythe eighth guru. became guru at five and died at eight after caring for the sick during an epidemic in delhi
11guru tegh bahadur1621-1675historythe ninth guru. gave his life for the religious freedom of others
12guru gobind singh1666-1708historythe tenth guru. poet and warrior who founded the khalsa and made the scripture the eternal guru
13mata sahib kaur1681-1747historywife of guru gobind singh. honored as the mother of the khalsa
14banda singh bahadur1670-1716historysikh military leader. led a revolt against mughal rule and gave land to the peasants who worked it
15ranjit singh1780-1839historymaharaja of the punjab. built a strong sikh empire, tolerant of all faiths, and covered the golden temple in gold
