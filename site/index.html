the history of the world

this site tells the story of everything that has ever happened, from the big bang to the present, in eleven parts. each part is a long chapter. read them in order, or jump to whatever you want.

in total there are 35,248 words of history, 531 dated events on the timeline, 8 topics, 205 people, 171 glossary terms, and 14 regions.

topics

8 shorter pages that each follow one thread through all of history:

  • writing: how people learned to store words outside their heads: from clay tokens to alphabets, paper, printing, and screens.
  • money and trade: debts in silver, the first coins, camel caravans and monsoon ships, paper money, and a world economy tied together by silver, slavery, and steam.
  • energy: every age of history has run on some source of power: muscle, fire, wind and water, coal, oil, the atom, and now the sun again.
  • disease and medicine: plagues have killed more people than wars. this is the story of how they spread, and how people slowly learned to fight back.
  • migration: humans have always been on the move: out of africa, across oceans, along the steppe, in chains, and in search of a better life.
  • belief: from careful burials and painted caves to the great world religions and modern secular states: how people have answered the biggest questions.
  • science: how many peoples, over thousands of years, built up a way of finding out how the world works by watching, measuring, and testing.
  • cities: from the first walled towns to megacities of thirty million: why people crowd together, and what it has cost and given them.
