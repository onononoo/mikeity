religions

every religion has its own account of how the world began, what has happened in it, and where it is going. this section gives each of {{ religions|length }} major faiths a small site of its own, with its story, a timeline, its people, and a glossary, laid out just like the rest of this site.

each timeline mixes two kinds of entry, and always says which is which:

  • tradition: the story as the faith itself tells it, in its scriptures and teachings. dates given here are traditional ones.
  • history: what historians and archaeologists can show from evidence, like the rest of this site's timeline.

the site does not take sides. believers read many of the traditional accounts in different ways, some literally and some not, and the pages try to say so.
